About Speaker

Michael Chen
is a cybersecurity expert and cloud technology specialist with over 12 years of experience in protecting digital infrastructures and building secure enterprise solutions. He has led security initiatives for international organizations, helping businesses strengthen their cyber defenses and adopt modern cloud technologies with confidence.

Known for his practical approach and engaging speaking style, Michael shares valuable insights on emerging security threats, risk management, and the future of cybersecurity. At TechFuture 2026, he aims to empower professionals with the knowledge and strategies needed to navigate today's rapidly evolving digital landscape.
